MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

The source is a single posed scene (console, memory card, both pad variants, two
plug ends, eight game jewel cases, two baked cords). It was split into five
per-part GLBs by Tools/glb/split_playstation.py:

  ps1_console.glb      consoles/playstation/
  ps1_memory_card.glb  consoles/playstation/
  ps1_plug.glb         controllers/playstation/   (the controller connector)

Both PADS from this scene were extracted and then DROPPED. Their four face
buttons, both shoulder pairs and SELECT/START are one connected surface (11,784
triangles in a single island), so no separation recovers them and no button can
be animated. RetroXR uses synthetic worlds' SCPH-1080 pad instead — see
../../controllers/playstation/LICENSE-playstation-controllers.txt.

TRADEMARK REMOVAL

  * Deleted the raised "PS" logo plate on the disc lid (object PSX|Lid.001,
    material LID_Logo), and with it the logo texture.
  * Cleared the "SONY" and "PlayStation" wordmarks from the console's
    Lid_Decals sheet. Nothing else was on that texture, so it is now flat.
  * Cleared the "SONY" and "PlayStation" wordmarks from the console's
    Lid_Buttons_Ports_Smooth sheet, which also carries functional legends. Only
    the two marks were erased; OPEN, POWER, RESET, MEMORY CARD and the port
    numbers 1 and 2 are untouched.
  * Cleared "PlayStation" and the PS logo glyph from the memory card's
    MCard_Decals sheet. "MEMORY CARD" is untouched.

  The four face-button symbols (triangle, circle, cross, square) are KEPT as
  functional control legends, on the same basis as the console's POWER/RESET
  legends: without them the pad's controls are unlabelled.

  The rear-panel legends are all functional and are kept: SERIAL I/O,
  PARALLEL I/O, AV MULTI OUT, VIDEO OUT, AUDIO OUT L (MONO) / R, RF DC OUT,
  AC IN.

THIRD-PARTY ARTWORK REMOVAL

  * Deleted all eight game jewel cases and their cover, booklet and disc art:
    Final Fantasy Tactics, Final Fantasy VII, Resident Evil, Twisted Metal 2,
    Legacy of Kain, Bushido Blade, Wild Arms 2, Breath of Fire III and Jet Moto.
    These are the publishers' artwork, not the model author's, and none of it is
    covered by this model's licence. Nine textures went with them.
  * Replaced the licensed character sticker on the memory card (Street Fighter's
    Chun-Li) with a blank white label.

GEOMETRY AND SCENE CHANGES

  * Deleted both baked controller cords (DS_Chord, ControlerStan_Chord) and the
    rubber band prop. RetroXR simulates every cord with a VerletRope.
  * Deleted the artist's discs. They measure 159.5 mm across against a CD's 120
    at any scale factor; RetroXR builds discs from MediaDimensions at runtime.
  * Rescaled the whole scene by 1/2.2732, undoing the display scale the author
    applied. This recovers the authored real-world dimensions: the console lands
    at 264.6 x 54.4 x 186.7 mm and the memory card at 38.2 x 7.4 x 56.0 mm.
  * Re-origined each part: centred on its own footprint and resting on y = 0.
  * Removed the display poses. The pads were yawed 24 and 42 degrees and tilted
    16 degrees on the render desk; the disc lid was modelled 50 degrees open.
    All are now axis-aligned, and the lid ships shut.
  * Rotated the plug 180 degrees so its connector faces +Z with the cord
    trailing -Z, and moved its origin to the housing's mating shoulder, which is
    the contract ControllerPlug.set_plug_mesh() reads.
  * Separated the console's single Buttons mesh into ButtonPower / ButtonReset /
    ButtonOpen, and its single FrontPorts mesh into Port1 / Port2 / MemCard1 /
    MemCard2, so each control can travel and each socket can be measured.
  * Renamed the shell's nodes from Sketchfab's "PSX|Name|Dupli|N" form to
    descriptive names (Lid, Buttons, PowerLight, FrontPorts, DiscCradle,
    LaserBed, Spindle, LaserTrolley, JackAC, ParallelPortCover, ShellBottom,
    ShellTop) so the moving parts can be bound by name.
  * Decimated from 1,681,436 triangles to ~34,000 across the parts kept, after a
    weld, with per-part floors so the button caps keep their shape.
    The console shell is otherwise unchanged geometry by the original author.
